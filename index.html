01 / Studio

Where craft meets coast.

NuConcepts is a Sri Lankan interior design and furniture studio creating spaces for boutique hotels, villas, restaurants and private residences. From first sketch to finished space, one team controls the process end to end.

That single line of responsibility is the point. Because we design the space and then build the furniture that goes into it, drawings do not get lost in translation between a designer, a supplier and a contractor — and the person who specified a joint is the person who cuts it.
