    Our story — Est. 2018

    Eight years of making in Sri Lanka.

    NuConcepts began in 2018 with a simple idea — design and making should belong together.

    Today, our Colombo workshop brings furniture to life from the first drawing to the final finish. Timber is selected, cut, joined, shaped, sanded, upholstered and finished by the same team responsible for turning an idea into something made to last.

    Designed here. Made here. Made for the space.

    Furniture doesn’t begin with the finished piece.

    Made for where it lives.

    Furniture should respond to its surroundings. Our materials, construction and finishes are considered around the space, climate and the way each piece will actually be used.

    01

    Made for the coast.

    Materials that belong here.

    Salt air, humidity and tropical conditions influence how we select timber, construct joinery and specify finishes.

    02

    Made to last.

    Construction is in the details.

    From timber selection and joinery to sanding and finishing, every detail is considered for how the furniture will live and age.

    03

    Made for hospitality.

    Beautiful furniture has to work.

    For hotels, villas and restaurants, comfort and appearance are only part of the brief. Durability, maintenance and everyday use shape the way we design each piece.
