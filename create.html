    Create with us

    Your idea. Our process.
    Made for your space.

    From a single custom piece to a complete furniture package, we make the process straightforward. One team manages it from the first conversation to the final installation.

    The path a project takes

    Eight stages. Jump to any of them.

    1. 01Share your requirements
    2. 02Quotation
    3. 03Site visit & measurement
    4. 04Materials & finishes
    1. 05Production drawings
    2. 06Manufacturing
    3. 07Delivery & installation
    4. 08Final handover
    01Share your requirements

    Tell us about your project.

    Send us your furniture requirements, architectural drawings, BOQ, reference images — or simply tell us what you would like to create.

    Whether it is a private residence, villa, hotel, restaurant or commercial space, our team will review your requirements and help establish the scope of work.

    Upload your drawings / BOQ / references →
    02Quotation

    Clear pricing from the beginning.

    Based on the information provided, we prepare a detailed quotation covering the furniture, quantities, specifications and proposed finishes.

    For larger projects, quotations can be organised by room, area or furniture category for easier review.

    Request a quotation →
    03Site visit & measurement

    We measure. We verify.

    Once the project proceeds, our team visits the site to verify dimensions and assess site conditions, access and installation requirements.

    For custom joinery and built-in furniture, final measurements are taken before production drawings are completed.

    04Materials & finishes

    Choose the details that make it yours.

    We work with you to finalise the materials and finishes for each piece.

    • Timber
    • Upholstery
    • Fabrics
    • Colours
    • Finishes
    • Hardware
    • Stone
    • Metal details

    Where required, physical samples are submitted for approval.

    05Production drawings

    See the details before we build.

    Before manufacturing, we prepare final production drawings for custom furniture and joinery where required.

    These drawings incorporate confirmed site measurements, dimensions, materials and construction details.

    Client approval

    Nothing proceeds to manufacturing until the required production details are approved.

    06Manufacturing

    Made in our Colombo workshop.

    Once approved, your furniture moves into production. Our team manages the process from fabrication and joinery through to finishing, upholstery and quality control.

    07Delivery & installation

    From our workshop to your space.

    Once production is complete, we coordinate delivery and installation with your project team.

    For larger projects, deliveries can be scheduled in stages according to site readiness and the overall project programme.

    08Final handover

    The finishing touch.

    Following installation, our team carries out a final inspection and attends to any required adjustments before project handover.

    From the first conversation to the final installation, one team manages the process.
