    Home / Work / Abode Ahangama

    Beachfront, detailed for the weather.

    Scope

    Guestrooms, public areas and F&B

    Services

    Interior design · Bespoke furniture · Hospitality fit-out

    Location

    Ahangama, Sri Lanka

    Completed

    2023

    Sitting directly on the beach, Abode needed an interior that could take the monsoon as well as the season: hardwoods and finishes selected for humidity and salt, and joinery detailed with movement in mind.

    The package covered guestrooms, the public areas and the F&B spaces, so the same material palette carries from the rooms through to where guests eat.

    Design, manufacture, delivery and installation were handled by one team from our Colombo workshop.
